Weeknight Red Lentil Soup

By Ines Duarte, 40 minutes, serves 4

This is the soup I make when the fridge is nearly empty: red lentils, an onion, a carrot, a tin of tomatoes and whatever spice is closest to hand. It thickens as it stands, so add water when you reheat it.
