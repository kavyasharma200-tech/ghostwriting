Community garden opens on Rua das Flores

Local news, 21 October 2026

Forty raised beds opened on Saturday on the former car park behind the market. Plots are free for residents of the parish for the first year, and a waiting list is already forming.

The garden is open daily from 8:00 to 20:00. Tools are kept in the shed by the north gate.

Volunteers will run a seed swap on the first Sunday of every month.
