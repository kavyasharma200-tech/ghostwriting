Brightwater 1.7 L Glass Kettle

Reviewed by Tom Achterberg, 3 October 2026

The Brightwater boils a full litre in about two minutes and forty seconds, which is ordinary for a 2200 W kettle. The glass body looks lovely on the counter but shows limescale within a week in hard-water areas.
