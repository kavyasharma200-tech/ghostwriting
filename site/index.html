    A person reads the pixels. An AI reads the text.

    Ghostwriting draws every page exactly as you'd see it, then checks each line of the text layer against what was actually drawn. A line with no ink under it, a line too small to read, a line off the edge of the page: those are written for the machine alone.

    1. White inktext the colour of the paper
    2. Whisper typeone or two points tall
    3. Over the edgeplaced outside the page
    4. Under a patchcovered by a shape on top
    5. Invisible modePDF text set to draw nothing
    6. Almost papera grey one shade off white
    7. Zero-widthcharacters with no width at all
    8. Tag charactersa whole message in invisible Unicode
    9. Hidden HTMLdisplay:none, comments, same-colour text

    The documents worth checking are the private ones: the CV you're sending, the paper under review, the contract in your inbox. Ghostwriting reads them with pdf.js and asks Gemma 3 1B about them on your own graphics card, so the only copy stays where it already was, and it keeps working with the Wi-Fi off once the model is cached.
